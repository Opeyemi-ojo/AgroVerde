FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

🌱 Do you work with smallholder farms?

Absolutely. Our services are designed to scale. We work with farms of all sizes — from 2-hectare family plots to 10,000-hectare commercial estates.

📋 How does a free consultation work?

We'll schedule a 45-minute call with one of our agronomists to understand your situation. You'll walk away with at least three actionable insights — regardless of whether you become a client.

🌍 Do you operate outside of Europe?

Yes — we have active teams in West Africa, East Africa, South Asia, and Latin America, with a new Southeast Asia office opening in late 2025.

💰 What does a typical engagement cost?

Costs vary significantly based on scope and location. We offer both project-based and retainer arrangements. Contact us and we'll provide a clear, itemised quote.

⏱️ How quickly can you deploy on-farm?

For urgent situations (crop failure, pest outbreak), we can typically dispatch a field specialist within 72 hours. Standard engagements are usually started within 2–3 weeks.

🎓 Do you offer training programmes?

Yes — we run both on-farm workshops and online courses for farm workers, managers, and cooperative leaders. Ask us about our AgroAcademy platform.
