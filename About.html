The AgroVerde Story

From a Single Farm to a Global Movement

AgroVerde was born in 2003 when agronomist Dr. Elena Vasquez and soil scientist Dr. Kwame Asante decided to leave their academic posts to do something radical: make world-class agricultural science accessible to small-scale farmers.

Their first project — a 45-hectare degraded coffee plantation in Guatemala — became the proof of concept that would define the next two decades. Using innovative companion planting, organic composting, and water-harvesting techniques, the plantation recovered in three seasons and became one of the most photographed sustainable farms in Central America.

Today, AgroVerde operates across 18 countries, employing over 320 specialists and supporting more than 840 farm partners with a full spectrum of agribusiness solutions.

Our Mission

To empower farming communities worldwide with the knowledge, technology, and resources needed to produce food sustainably — improving livelihoods, protecting ecosystems, and feeding a growing global population without compromise.

We achieve this through direct on-farm partnerships, accessible education, and the development of scalable, science-driven solutions that work in the real world, not just in theory.

🔭

Our Vision

A world in which every farmer — regardless of geography, land size, or financial resources — has access to the same quality of agronomic support as the largest agribusinesses. A future where farming restores rather than depletes, where rural communities thrive, and where food security is genuinely universal.

We are building toward a global food system that is regenerative by design and equitable by default.

Our Journey

Two Decades of Growing Impact

Key milestones that chart how a two-person startup became a globally respected agribusiness.

2003

Founded in Guatemala

Dr. Vasquez and Dr. Asante launch AgroVerde with their first client — a degraded coffee plantation that became their blueprint for regenerative transformation.

2007

West Africa Expansion

Open our Accra office and begin consulting work with cocoa and cassava cooperatives in Ghana, Ivory Coast, and Cameroon.

2011

ISO 14001 Certification

Achieve environmental management certification, cementing our commitment to measurable, audited sustainability standards.

2015

AgroVerde SmartIrrigate™ Launch

Launch our proprietary AI-assisted irrigation management platform, which goes on to serve clients across 12 countries in its first three years.

2019

500th Farm Partner Milestone

Celebrate the onboarding of our 500th farm partner, representing over 40,000 farming families in 14 countries.

2022

Global Sustainable Agriculture Award

Recognised at the World AgriTech Innovation Summit as the leading agribusiness consultancy for ecological impact.

2025

840+ Partners & 18 Countries

Reaching our largest ever footprint and launching our Carbon Farming Fund to help smallholders access carbon credit markets.
